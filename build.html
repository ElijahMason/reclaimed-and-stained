Build

The board already served. Put it back to work.

People ask for this wood for construction, patios, decking, and similar building. The next job has to fit the piece.

Name the job

Say what you are building and what you need the piece to do. A deck surface, a patio edge, a repair, or similar work all belong on the brief. Add a rough length if you know it. Add your timing.

What a reading shows

A reading notes the remaining thickness, the checks you can see, whether the fasteners came out, and that the shop kept the piece because it can still do a job.

What your builder confirms

Your builder confirms a piece fits the job. The shop does not mark the wood as structurally graded. When a piece has to carry a load or span a gap, the builder makes that call.

Write the brief

Choose Ask for wood. Describe the job and the timing. When you submit, the page copies a brief and shows it.
