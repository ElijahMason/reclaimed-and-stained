Intake

Reclaimed & Stained takes donated reclaimed wood and prepares it for people who need it for construction, patios, decking, and similar building. The shop is a 501(c)(3) in Milwaukie, Oregon.

A builder confirms the piece. The shop does not grade wood for structure.
