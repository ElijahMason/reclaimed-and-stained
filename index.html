Reclaimed & Stained

The board already served. Put it back to work.

Reclaimed & Stained is a 501(c)(3) in Milwaukie, Oregon. The shop takes donated reclaimed wood and prepares it for people who need it for construction, patios, decking, and similar building.

The standard

Pull the nails. Read the board. Keep pieces that can still do a job. Pass on what cannot.

A builder confirms a piece fits the job. The shop does not call a piece structurally graded.

Read how a board is kept

Stain card

When a kept piece needs a finish, the shop stains it and names the color from this card. Each name is a shop label for a flat color. These names are not brands.

  • Nail Iron
  • Dry Loft
  • Deck Clay
  • Cut Sap
  • Hard Pitch
  • Wet Board
