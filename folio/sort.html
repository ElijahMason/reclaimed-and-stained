RS-02

Sort

The shop sorts donated reclaimed wood so you can match a piece to a job. This sheet names the categories. It does not count pieces.

What the columns name

what came in
The kind of reclaimed wood.
nails
Fasteners that came with the wood.
fit
How the piece sits. Fit is not a structural grade. A builder confirms the piece.
where it can go
Construction, a patio, decking, or similar building.

Amounts and counts stay off this sheet. Blank lines mean the week is not filled with fake numbers.
