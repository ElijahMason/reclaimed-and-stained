The board already served. Put it back to work.

Two paths sit on this sheet. You can leave wood, or you can ask for wood.

Leave wood

You have reclaimed wood and you want it to work again. Write what you have. If you know the last job, name it: a deck, a patio, other construction, or similar building. Rough sizes help. Say whether fasteners are still in the wood. Say when you can part with it.

The shop pulls the nails, reads the board, keeps pieces that can still do a job, and passes on what cannot. On the contact sheet, choose Leave wood. When you submit, the page copies a brief you can keep.

Write a leave-wood brief

Ask for wood

You need wood for construction, a patio, decking, or similar building. Write the job, what you need the wood to do, and your timing. You are asking for pieces the shop has kept after a reading. Your builder confirms a piece fits the job.

On the contact sheet, choose Ask for wood. When you submit, the page copies a brief you can keep.
